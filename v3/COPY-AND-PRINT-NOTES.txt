TANDPAY — ESCROW BOOTH ARTWORK, LAUNCH EDITION, VERSION 3

Finished artwork: nominal 2000 × 2000 mm at 1:1 scale.
Headline: Escrow protection. For social commerce.
Core story: Pay → Protect → Confirm → Release.
Buyer protection: Inspect before confirming.
Seller protection: Verify payment before dispatch.
Scan invitation: SCAN TO GET STARTED / tandpay.com.
Funds qualification: Partner-managed funds. Transaction terms apply.

This event edition assumes TandPay will have launched by the event, as Bahati
instructed. It contains no early-access badge and does not assert a launch date
or that the service has already launched today. Previous versions are retained
in Git history and earlier releases; use this version for the current artwork.

QR CODE
Exact encoded destination: https://tandpay.com
The live HTTPS destination was checked and returned HTTP 200 after redirecting
to https://www.tandpay.com/. The QR encodes the requested root-domain URL.
No third-party QR service, shortener, tracking redirect or embedded secret.
Generated locally with qrcode 1.5.4; error correction Q; 25 × 25 modules;
four-module white quiet zone on every side; solid black modules on white.
The QR occupies a 320 × 320 mm square in the artwork.
Independently decoded with jsQR from the standalone QR PNG, the complete
2000 px artwork preview, and the QR region of the 8000 px artwork export.
All three decoded exactly https://tandpay.com.

FILES
tandpay-escrow-booth-2000mm.pdf: one-page vector PDF; Chrome rounds the physical
page to approximately 1999.91 × 1999.91 mm. No raster images embedded.
tandpay-escrow-booth-2000mm.svg: self-contained editable vector composition,
including an embedded Manrope font resource and the vector QR pattern.
tandpay-escrow-booth-8000px.png: 8000 × 8000 pixels, equivalent to 101.6 pixels
per inch when assigned a 2000 × 2000 mm print size; exported from vector artwork.
tandpay-escrow-booth-preview.png: 2000 × 2000 pixel screen preview.
Standalone QR SVG, PNG and encoding metadata are included in assets/.

Colour: RGB. No bleed, seam or attachment allowance has been assumed. The
printer should confirm the required bleed, finishing, colour profile and image
resolution. Use the vector PDF when the printer accepts scalable vector artwork.
Maintain the white QR quiet zone and black/white contrast during production.

BUILD AND VERIFICATION
make_tandpay_qr.cjs generates QR assets using qrcode.
build_escrow_launch_artwork.py reuses build_escrow_artwork.py, writes v3/,
removes the access label and places the QR and website address.
verify_tandpay_qr.cjs independently decodes exported artwork using jsqr and sharp.
Node build/verification dependencies: qrcode 1.5.4, jsqr 1.4.0, and sharp.
Provide these via Node's module search path or a local npm installation.
No new AI-generated raster imagery was used in version 3.
