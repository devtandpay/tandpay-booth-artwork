TANDPAY EXHIBITION WALL — VERSION 1

Finished artwork size: 2000 × 2000 mm. PDFs are supplied at 1:1 scale.
Colour: RGB. No bleed, trim marks or finishing allowance have been assumed.
Ask the printer to confirm their bleed, attachment/seam allowance, colour profile,
and whether their workflow accepts the supplied PDF. Convert to their requested
CMYK profile in the prepress workflow if required.

EDITIONS
1. tandpay-booth-vector-2000mm.pdf
   Entirely vector artwork, including vector lettering and a flat-colour mark traced
   from the website logo. Recommended for the sharpest reproduction at 2 metres.
   The traced mark is a production adaptation, not an original vector brand master.

2. tandpay-booth-illustrated-2000mm.pdf
   Vector lettering and mark with an AI-generated editorial seller illustration.
   The transparent illustration is 1254 × 1254 px and is placed 850 mm wide
   (approximately 37.5 pixels per inch at final size). This is an illustrated proof;
   obtain the printer's acceptance or a suitable higher-resolution source before
   committing this edition to a large-format print run. Upscaling alone does not
   restore missing detail.

The PNGs are 2000 × 2000 pixel screen previews, not 300 dpi print exports.
The SVGs retain editable vector lettering and embed their font/image resources.
Some design applications may need Manrope installed when editing the SVG.
The PDFs preserve the rendered lettering as vector graphics for consistent appearance.
